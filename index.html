About Us

Established in 1997, Variopack Prima specializes in providing highquality processing and packaging machinery, mainly European for a wide range of industries in Indonesia, including F&B, FMCG, Pharma, Cosmetics, and personal care.

With experience dating back to 1991, we have built a robust portfolio of exclusive partnerships with leading machine manufacturers. This allows us to offer tailored, innovative solutions that precisely meet the unique needs of each client and project. At Variopack Prima, our commitment is to empower your business with the best technology and expertise available.
